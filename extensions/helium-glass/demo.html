This page starts with ordinary opaque CSS. Helium Glass reads its palette and gives each surface a different level of transparency.

01 / SURFACES

A tint, not a fade.

The original surface color stays. Only the background alpha changes; these words keep their normal opacity.

02 / GRADIENTS

Keep the soft edges.

Neutral gradient stops become translucent too. Background photographs and media are kept intact.

Strong accent colors stay vivid.
03 / CONTROLS

Still feels familiar.

Inputs, buttons, and editable regions retain their own surfaces.

Not seeing the desktop? Enable Helium's transparent web content setting, load Helium Glass, and reload this page. Local files also need “Allow access to file URLs” in the extension's details. Try the theme switch, add a card, then disable the extension to see the original styling.
